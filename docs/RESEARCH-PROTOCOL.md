# Research protocol notes

These notes describe the current proposed workflow. They are not ethics approval or a finalized study protocol.

## Proposed allocation
- 540 scored items: 270 real Kannada words and 270 nonwords.
- Six fixed sessions of 90 items each.
- Every session contains 45 real words and 45 nonwords.
- Three visits, provisionally two sessions per visit.
- Five practice items before each session.
- A short standardized break after every 15 scored trials.

## Decisions requiring research-team confirmation
1. Are five practice items repeated before every session or only once per participant?
2. Should breaks occur after exactly 15, 30, 45, 60 and 75 trials?
3. Is item order fixed across participants or randomized within each session?
4. Can interrupted sessions resume? How are partial runs handled?
5. What age/grade groups and language-exposure criteria apply?
6. What psychometric model, calibration and score interpretation will be used?

## Data collection boundary
The current frontend is a preview. It stores trial records in browser local storage and exports CSV. It is not a secure shared research database. Do not use identifiable or operational participant data until authentication, role-based access, secure persistence, auditability, retention, backups and institutional approvals are implemented.
